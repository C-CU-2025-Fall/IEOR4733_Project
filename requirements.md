## [cite_start]Proposal Deck [cite: 13]

[cite_start]Students must: [cite: 14]
* [cite_start]Select a research paper (from provided list OR self-selected) OR propose a new research direction [cite: 15]
* [cite_start]Present a structured introduction deck [cite: 16]

### [cite_start]Approval Requirement [cite: 17]
[cite_start]If students: [cite: 18]
* [cite_start]Choose a paper NOT on the provided list [cite: 19]
* [cite_start]OR propose a new research idea [cite: 20]

[cite_start]They must receive approval from the Professor before proceeding. [cite: 21]

### Deck Structure
[cite_start]The deck that will be submitted in the mid-term should be approximately 4 slides and should answer the following questions and points: [cite: 22]
* [cite_start]What is the topic? [cite: 23]
* [cite_start]Brief Introduction of the topic. [cite: 24]
* [cite_start]Overview of Literature and the Methods. [cite: 25]
* [cite_start]Strategy Overview – This can consist of Data, Methodology, and Evaluation Metrics [cite: 26]
* [cite_start]Identified Weakness in existing Methods [cite: 27]
* [cite_start]Tentative Proposed Timeline of the Project or a Planned Extension of the Project – This can consist of what technology is being used or what strategy is being changed? [cite: 28]

## Project Approaches
[cite_start]There are two approaches for the project – Reproduce / Extend an Existing Paper, or Propose a New Research Area. [cite: 29]

### [cite_start]Option A – Reproduce / Extend an Existing Project [cite: 30]
[cite_start]Students may: [cite: 31]
* [cite_start]Reproduce results [cite: 32]
* [cite_start]Improve methodology [cite: 33]
* [cite_start]Stress-test assumptions [cite: 34]
* [cite_start]Extend to new markets / data [cite: 35]

[cite_start]If reproducing a paper - They must deliver a fully working trading system pipeline, not just a notebook. [cite: 36]

* [cite_start]Differences vs original [cite: 41]
* [cite_start]Robustness checks [cite: 42]
* [cite_start]Regime sensitivity [cite: 43]
* [cite_start]Risk diagnostics [cite: 44]

[cite_start]**Deployed Application - Must include:** [cite: 45]

* [cite_start]Backtest engine [cite: 47]
* [cite_start]Transaction cost modeling [cite: 48]
* [cite_start]Performance dashboard [cite: 49]
* [cite_start]Risk metrics [cite: 50]
* [cite_start]Ability to run new simulations [cite: 51]

[cite_start]**Acceptable formats:** [cite: 52]

* [cite_start]Interactive dashboard [cite: 54]
